Every night resting will heal one wound level.  For instance, a character with a Serious Wound would heal to a Light Wound after one night of rest.  Unfortunately, poisoned wounds can not be healed this way.

## War Council
Instead of resting, two or more characters can hold a War Council.  This council requires the drinking of at least two bottles of wine or casks of beer.  After the council, roll a d6 as you have now come up with a "Cunning Plan".  This number is stored for future use and may be used in place of any die roll.
