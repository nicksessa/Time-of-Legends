## Luck of the Day

To reflect the whims of Fate, each day a roll is made to determine how good or bad you day is going.  The roll is made using a d6 and this number sets a target number for that day.

When making a Luck test, roll a d6.  If the number rolled is equal to or greater than the Luck-of-the-Day, then the character is successful in the attempt.

The Luck-of-the-Day roll is made only once per turn and should be noted.  Any situations requiring a Luck Test during that turn must be made against this number. It will not change during the turn.
