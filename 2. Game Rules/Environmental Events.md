## Haunted Mists

Description: You catch glimpses of the faces of long dead adventurers in the mists that fill the crypt.

>Note
> If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until an new Environment Card is drawn.

Effect: Undead Monsters gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls.

##  Spirit of Doom

Description: A grim specter materializes nearby, bringing portents of impending doom.

>Note
> If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until an new Environment Card is drawn.

Effect: Whenever a Hero discards a Treasure Card, he takes 1 damage.

## Clear the Air

Effect: Discard an Environment Card in play.

## Music of the Damned

Description: The haunting sound of an organ echoes through the crypts, sending shivers down your spine.

>Note
> If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until an new Environment Card is drawn.

Effect: Whenever a Hero would place a new Monster, that Hero draws two Monster Cards and chooses the Monster worth the most experience. (toughest monster)  He places that Monster and discards the other.

## Deadly Shadows

Description: Voices from the shadows whisper horrifying secrets about your companions.

>Note
> If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until an new Environment Card is drawn.

Effect: Whenever the active Hero ends his or her turn on a tile that has another Hero on it, the active Hero takes 1 damage.

## Blood Fog

Description: A mystical reddish fog brings out the inner animal in the living and the dead.

>Note
> If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until an new Environment Card is drawn.

Effect: Whenever a Hero or a Monster attacks and rolls a natural 17 or higher, the target of the attack takes 1 additional damage.

## Bat Swarm

Description: A horde of shrieking bats flutter through the catacombs, blocking your sight.

>Note
> If there is an Environment Card already in play, discard it. This Environment remains in play until an new Environment Card is drawn.

Effect: Whenever a Hero attacks a monster that is not on his tile, that Hero makes the attack roll twice and uses the lower result.
